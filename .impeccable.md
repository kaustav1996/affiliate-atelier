## Design Context

### Users

ScentForge Atelier is used by hackathon judges, technical evaluators, affiliate creators, and eCommerce operators. They need to see a polished commerce demo, understand the generated storefront boundary, and verify that validation orders do not affect live earnings.

### Brand Personality

Luxurious, precise, imaginative, quietly technical.

### Aesthetic Direction

Premium fragrance commerce with an editorial atelier feel. Public storefronts use warm ivory, ink, forest green, muted gold, rose, and amber. Product surfaces are calmer and more structured, with terminal-like validation logs and legible operational metrics.

### Design Principles

- Make the first viewport unmistakably about perfume commerce.
- Keep dashboards structured, readable, and trustworthy.
- Make generated storefronts feel visually distinct while preserving platform checkout safety.
- Show validation status as proof, not decoration.
- Avoid generic AI SaaS tropes, crypto dashboards, and heavy glass effects.
