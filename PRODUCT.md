# ScentForge Atelier

## Product

ScentForge Atelier is a fictional affiliate commerce platform for premium perfume creators.

## Audience

- Hackathon judges evaluating the working demo and technical quality
- eCommerce operators looking for affiliate storefront workflows
- Affiliate creators who want a storefront that matches their audience
- Technical evaluators checking persistence, auth, validation, and Codex CLI usage

## Brand Voice

Luxurious, precise, imaginative, and quietly technical.

## Register

ScentForge uses a mixed register:

- Brand surfaces are editorial and sensory: the landing store, affiliate storefronts, generated drafts, and success states.
- Product surfaces are calm and operational: dashboard metrics, validation logs, publish gates, and the Atelier editor.

## Anti-References

- Generic purple AI SaaS
- Crypto dashboard styling
- Bland Shopify clone layouts
- Heavy glassmorphism
- Card grids without hierarchy or editorial presence

## Core Promise

Every affiliate gets a storefront that feels hand-made for their audience, generated by Codex and validated before it goes live.
